TAW Player for Mac (test build)
===============================

1. Drag "TAW Player" into your Applications folder (or anywhere you like).

2. The first time, double-click it. Your Mac will say it can't check the
   app ("Apple could not verify..."), because this test build isn't
   registered with Apple. Click Done (not Move to Bin).

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the
   Security section, and click "Open Anyway" next to "TAW Player was
   blocked". Confirm with your password. (On older Macs: right-click the
   app, choose Open, then Open again.)

After that it opens normally with a double-click.

Using it: open a folder of music or drop it on the window, then press
Play (or Space). It plays FLAC, MP3, WAV, AIFF, ALAC, AAC and Ogg Vorbis,
reads only, and never changes your files. The media keys on the keyboard
work too.

This is an early test for a friend; please say what works and what
doesn't (and which Mac and macOS version it's on).
